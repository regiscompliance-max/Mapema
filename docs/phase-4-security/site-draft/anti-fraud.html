How Mapema checks invoices and pays suppliers early

Mapema is a buyer-led early-payment service. A supplier is paid early only after the buyer has confirmed the invoice and signed an irrevocable undertaking to pay Mapema in full on the due date. Mapema is not yet licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya as a non-deposit-taking credit provider. We do not provide credit until we are licensed.
