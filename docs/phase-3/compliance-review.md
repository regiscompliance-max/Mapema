# Phase 3 compliance review

**[Compliance & Risk Analyst]** 18 Nov 2026. Scope: the Phase 3 surface (ERP imports, M-Pesa B2C payouts, bulk tools, payout batches, multi-lender funding tags and per-funder packs) against the **CBK (Non-Deposit Taking Credit Providers) Regulations 2026** and the **Data Protection Act 2019** (with the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act where M-Pesa is concerned). Regulation numbers follow the Phase 0 review, which relied on law-firm commentary. **They have not been checked against the gazetted text**; ⚖️ marks items for Kenyan counsel. This is not legal advice. Mapema is **not yet licensed**; nothing here is live.

## Items for Kenyan counsel (Phase 3)

1. M-Pesa B2C as a disbursement channel: CBK notice/approval, Safaricom terms (P3-C2).
2. Paying a company supplier into a director's personal M-Pesa wallet versus a company till/paybill; AML treatment of receiver-name mismatches (P3-C3).
3. ERP data pull: clause in the programme agreement, supplier notice, controller/processor roles, foreign hosting (P3-C4, P3-C9).
4. Named loan tapes for funders and the confidentiality/privacy basis (P3-C5).
5. Treatment of debt facilities under the NDTCP licensing and capital rules (P3-C6).
