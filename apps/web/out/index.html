Get paid for approved invoices in days, not months

Mapema pays suppliers early on invoices their buyer has approved. The buyer pays on the usual date. Suppliers see every shilling of cost before they accept.

How it works

  1. 1.Your buyer approves your invoice and signs that it will pay in full on the due date.
  2. 2.You see the exact advance, fees and balance, and accept with one tap.
  3. 3.Mapema sends 80% of the invoice to your bank account.
  4. 4.Your buyer pays Mapema on the due date. You get the balance within 1 business day.
